> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Deliver the complete v0.8.0 weapon-modification milestone with per-instance weapon craftsmanship, compatible installed upgrades, deterministic final profiles, persistence, UI, interoperability, and printable output.

**Architecture:** Extend the versioned equipment catalogue with source-grounded craftsmanship and upgrade effect data.
